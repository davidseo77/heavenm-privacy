Privacy Policy

Application: HeavenM Global Listing Automation

Last updated: October 3, 2026

HeavenM Global Listing Automation is an internal commerce automation application operated by HeavenM. It is currently used for HeavenM's own eBay seller account and is not offered as a public consumer application.

1. Information We Access

When an authorized eBay account grants permission, HeavenM Global Listing Automation may access information made available through eBay APIs within the OAuth permissions granted by the account holder.

This may include:

  • eBay account authorization information;
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens;
  • inventory and offer information;
  • product identifiers, catalog information and product metadata;
  • listing-related information required for HeavenM's commerce workflow.

HeavenM does not request or store an eBay user's password.

2. Purpose of Use

Information obtained through eBay APIs is used for HeavenM's internal commerce operations, including product identification, catalog validation, inventory-related processing, listing preparation, product image validation, product-data verification and related automation.

The application currently requests the eBay sell.inventory OAuth permission for authorized eBay API access.

HeavenM's current application policy prohibits automated use of eBay write APIs. Current integration use is limited to authorized read and validation workflows unless that policy is explicitly changed in the future.

3. Data Sharing

HeavenM does not sell eBay account information or eBay-derived personal information to advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Information may be processed by infrastructure or service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate, secure, maintain or support the application.

4. Data Storage and Security

HeavenM applies reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect authentication credentials and information obtained through eBay APIs.

Access tokens, refresh tokens, client secrets and other authentication credentials are treated as confidential and are not intentionally exposed in public repositories, logs or public web pages.

5. Data Retention

Information obtained through eBay APIs is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for authorized business purposes, operational continuity, security, troubleshooting or applicable legal obligations.

6. eBay Authorization and Revocation

Access to protected eBay account information occurs only after the eBay account holder grants authorization through eBay's OAuth consent process.

The account holder may revoke the application's authorization through the applicable eBay account settings. HeavenM will cease using revoked authorization credentials.

7. Changes to This Policy

HeavenM may update this Privacy Policy when the application's functions, data practices, legal requirements or eBay API integration change. The current version and revision date will be published on this page.

8. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy or HeavenM Global Listing Automation may be directed to HeavenM, the operator of this application. Additional public contact information may be published here as HeavenM's public web services are established.
